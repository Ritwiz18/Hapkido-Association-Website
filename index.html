About Us

Empowering Uttarakhand through the ancient Korean martial art of Hapkido since 2010.

What is Hapkido?

Hapkido is a dynamic Korean martial art focused on joint locks, throws, kicks, and strikes. Unlike purely offensive styles, Hapkido emphasises redirecting force and achieving control with minimal effort — making it ideal for all ages and fitness levels.

The Uttarakhand Sports Hapkido Association (USHA) is the premier governing body for Hapkido in Uttarakhand, affiliated with national and international federations. We promote the art through structured training, state championships, and community outreach.
